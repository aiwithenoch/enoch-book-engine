Automate the repeat

Keep the handoff moving

Let's say an email arrives, a class is booked, or a reminder is due. If you keep checking by hand, the same small job keeps asking for your attention.

You've connected calendars, inboxes, messaging, and local tools to move those jobs forward. Trigger, action, and failure case are enough to start, and the payoff is time returned to the work that needs you.

Automate the repeat, keep the judgment.
