# Blocks

The first edition is a short working portrait of Enoch and AI With Enoch. Each page
holds one idea, one visual, and one action.

## Part 1 · The work

#### AI With Enoch
Identity · diagram
- **What:** Enoch's work teaches practical AI from Ghana to creators, students, and business owners.
- **Use when:** a reader wants to understand the work behind the name.
- **Action:** Start with the result I need. Then show me the smallest AI step that gets me there in plain language.
- **Band:** diagram (real work flows through practical teaching into a useful result)

#### Practical AI
Teaching · diagram
- **What:** The work turns a confusing tool into a next action a real person can use.
- **Use when:** someone knows AI matters but does not know what to do with it.
- **Action:** Take one real task. Ask AI for one useful next step in plain language, then do that step.
- **Band:** diagram (before and after: too many tools versus one clear next move)

#### 24 HOURS OF AI
Event · diagram
- **What:** A 24-hour video-first run trained 6,371 people.
- **Use when:** teaching needs reach, stamina, and a format people can join.
- **Action:** Choose one practical AI topic. Teach it live or on video until one real person can use it.
- **Band:** diagram (a 24-hour timeline ending in a wider room)

## Part 2 · The system

#### Classroom system
Education · diagram
- **What:** Teaching includes the booking, calendar, meeting link, reminder, class, and follow-up around the lesson.
- **Use when:** a growing class has repeated coordination work.
- **Action:** Map my path from booking to follow-up. Show every handoff and automate the repeatable ones.
- **Band:** diagram (booking flows through the class into follow-up)

#### Automate the repeat
Systems · diagram
- **What:** Enoch connects calendars, inboxes, messaging, and local tools so repeated work can move on a trigger.
- **Use when:** small checks and reminders keep pulling attention away from teaching.
- **Action:** Name the trigger, the action, and what should happen if the automation fails.
- **Band:** diagram (new event flows through a workflow into a timely reminder)

#### Build what repeats
Product · diagram
- **What:** Repeated work around tasks, bookings, reminders, and follow-up can become a small system.
- **Use when:** the same task returns and has to be rebuilt by hand.
- **Action:** Find one task I repeat. Build the smallest tool that handles its complete loop.
- **Band:** diagram (manual repetition becomes a reusable tool)

## Part 3 · The reach

#### One idea, many formats
Media · diagram
- **What:** An idea can move through video, motion, captions, documents, and PDFs without losing its core point.
- **Use when:** one lesson needs to reach people in more than one format.
- **Action:** Take one lesson and plan its video, page, and checklist versions without changing the core point.
- **Band:** diagram (one idea branches into a video and a book page)

#### Memory is infrastructure
Knowledge · diagram
- **What:** Durable context lets ongoing work continue without starting every session from zero.
- **Use when:** projects accumulate decisions, current state, and next steps.
- **Action:** Save the decision, the current state, and the next step where I can search them later.
- **Band:** diagram (conversation becomes durable memory, then a better next session)

#### Proof in public
Recognition · diagram
- **What:** Public proof includes Tech Creator of the Year at the 2025 Ghana Creators Awards, Parliament AI training, and a 24 HOURS OF AI run that trained 6,371 people.
- **Use when:** trust should rest on demonstrated work.
- **Action:** Keep a simple record of the work I complete, the people I help, and the proof I can show.
- **Band:** diagram (completed work becomes proof, then trust)
