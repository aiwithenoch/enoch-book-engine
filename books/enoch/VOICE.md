# Voice

This is a short working portrait of Enoch and AI With Enoch. It should sound like one
builder explaining the work to another person, with enough detail to feel real and no
claims that cannot be supported.

## Who we are writing for

Someone who has seen Enoch's work or is trying to understand what sits behind the name.
They may be a creator, student, business owner, or builder. They want the working idea,
not a polished legend.

## The rules

- Use simple words and contractions.
- No em dashes. Use commas or full stops.
- Keep the tone direct, practical, and human.
- Describe the work before praising it.
- Use verified details from Enoch's working history. Do not add private contact details,
  credentials, client information, or invented stories.
- Give each page one idea and one action.

## The opening

Open with "Let's say…" and put the reader inside a real work problem before naming the
idea.

## The closer

End with one short sentence that could be repeated tomorrow.

## The action box

The action should be something small and practical. It can be a prompt Enoch or the
reader could give an AI, but it must not pretend that a tool did work it did not do.
